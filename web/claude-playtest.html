Move recipes

P-speed
Maker styles: hold run on flat ground until the P meter fills
Wall jump
Every maker style: hold toward a wall while falling, then jump
Vines
Hold ↑ to grab, Z to jump off (C spins off in Island)
Triple jump
Modern: jump again right as you land, three times while running
Long jump
Athletic: run, then X + C (or run, hold ↓, jump)
Backflip
Athletic: hold ↓ for about a second, then jump
Roll / crawl
Athletic: crouch, then C to roll or ←→ to crawl
Slowfall
Maker styles: hold jump while falling
